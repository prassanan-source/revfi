Setup Guide
  1. Go to developer.squareup.com/apps
  2. Create an app (or open existing)
  3. Under Credentials → copy your Access Token
    Use Sandbox token for testing, Production for live
  4. Under Locations → copy your Location ID
  5. Click Save & Test Connection
  6. For auto-paid detection, add a webhook:
    URL: https://yourdomain.com/webhooks/square
    Event: invoice.payment_made
How Invoice Sending Works

When you send via Square:

  1. Karyva.ai creates a Square Customer (or finds existing)
  2. Creates an Order for the invoice amount
  3. Creates and publishes a Square Invoice
  4. Square emails a branded payment page to your client
  5. Client pays by card, ACH, Cash App, or Afterpay
  6. Square fires a webhook → Karyva.ai marks the invoice Paid automatically

Square charges 3.3% + 30¢ per invoice payment (US). Funds deposit to your Square account.
